## 4. Full Description
Step into the founder's shoes in this retro 90s-style choose-your-path business adventure!

**Spotlight Business Play** puts you at the helm of iconic business ventures where every decision determines your fate. Experience authentic business strategy through concise narrative scenes, incoming memos and reports, and immediate choices (A, B, C, D or keys 1-4).

### Key Features
- **90s Choose-Your-Path Adventure:** Classic gamebook gameplay reimagined for modern business. No complicated menus or arbitrary points—just pure storytelling and executive decisions.
- **Single Winning Spine:** Exactly one chain of sound business moves leads to breakthrough victory. Can you spot the strategic traps and find the path to glory?
- **Clear Business Lessons:** Every wrong decision branches quickly into a fatal pitfall, followed by an actionable breakdown of the business principle violated (cash runway, pricing margins, governance, product focus).
- **Offline & Instant:** Works 100% offline out of the box with bundled story content. Automatically discovers new community episodes over the air from GitHub.
- **100% Free & Private:** No accounts, no subscriptions, no advertisements, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. All progress stays safely on your machine.
- **Accessible & Clean:** High-contrast light and dark themes, 5 scalable font sizes, full keyboard shortcuts, and clean, legible design.

Whether you are an entrepreneur, student, executive, or retro game enthusiast, Spotlight Business Play delivers an engaging 35+ minute strategy adventure every time.
