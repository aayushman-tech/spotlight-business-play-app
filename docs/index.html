Spotlight Business Play Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 | Application: Spotlight Business Play for Windows
Summary: Spotlight Business Play is 100% free and private. No personal information is collected, no analytics or trackers are used, no advertising is served, and all game progress stays on your machine.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Spotlight Business Play does not collect, record, transmit, or sell personal data:

  • No user accounts: You do not need to register, sign in, or provide an email address.
  • No telemetry or analytics: No tracking SDKs, third-party beacons, or telemetry services exist in the application.
  • No advertisements: There are no advertisements, marketing trackers, or affiliate scripts.
  • No payments or purchases: The app is completely free without in-app purchases.

2. Network Communications

The only network requests made by Spotlight Business Play are:

  • Story Content Updates: When you open the game or check for new stories, the app queries the public GitHub repository (raw.githubusercontent.com) to check for newly published story files.
  • Sandboxing: These requests are performed strictly by the background main process via HTTPS GET. The game interface renderer has no external network access and cannot connect to any external server.
  • No outgoing telemetry: No device telemetry, IP logs, or gameplay statistics are transmitted to our servers.

3. Local Data Storage

All game progress, choices, completed story history, and settings (such as theme and text size) are saved locally on your computer in the standard Windows Application Data folder:

%APPDATA%\Spotlight Business Play\

This data never leaves your device. You can erase all data at any time from the in-game Settings screen via the "Reset all progress" button.

4. Children's Privacy

Because Spotlight Business Play collects no personal information, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international data protection laws. It is safe for players of all ages.

5. Source Code & Verification

Spotlight Business Play is open source. You can inspect the application source code and story schemas anytime:

  • App Repository: https://github.com/aayushman-tech/spotlight-business-play-app
  • Stories Repository: https://github.com/aayushman-tech/spotlight-business-play-stories
