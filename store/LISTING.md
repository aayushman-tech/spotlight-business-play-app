## 4. Full Description
Step into the executive suite where every decision has quantifiable consequences.

**Boardroom Logic** is a free, interactive decision-making drama that tests your business instincts and logical rigor. As a corporate leader, you will navigate crisis management, balance sheet trade-offs, high-stakes negotiations, and boardroom politics.

### What Makes Boardroom Logic Different?
- **Realistic In-Box Simulation:** Decisions don't appear in vacuum text boxes. Information arrives as corporate emails, memos, urgent phone calls, news alerts, contracts, and financial reports.
- **Data-Driven Reasoning:** Analyze embedded balance sheets, burn-rate charts, pricing elasticity tables, and unit-economic KPI tiles before making your call.
- **Pure Logic Over Luck:** Every scenario is 100% deterministic. Outrageous gambles and tempting fallacies (sunk cost, opportunity cost neglect, survivorship bias) will be exposed in the post-game debrief.
- **Dynamic Content Updates:** New episodes and case studies arrive seamlessly from our community-driven story archive without needing to update the app.
- **100% Free & Private:** No accounts, no subscriptions, no ads, no in-app purchases, and zero telemetry. All progress stays safely on your machine.

Whether you are an aspiring founder, MBA student, finance professional, or lover of thoughtful narrative puzzles, Boardroom Logic offers deep, analytical gameplay in every session.

## 5. Key Features
1. **Interactive Corporate Drama:** High-stakes business scenarios featuring diverse characters, conflicting motives, and branching storylines.
2. **Rich Visual Exhibits:** Inspect interactive data exhibits including SVG financial charts, balance sheet tables, and real-time KPI metric monitors.
3. **Comprehensive Executive Debrief:** Receive a detailed logic score (0–100), corporate rank (from Intern to CEO), and actionable analysis of cognitive biases.
4. **Episodic Case Files:** "Today's Story" hub delivers fresh 30+ minute narrative challenges, plus a permanent playable archive.
5. **Accessibility & Customization:** Full keyboard navigation (keys 1-4 for choices), high-contrast dark/light modes, adjustable typography, and reduced-motion support.
6. **Zero Tracking or Monetization:** Fully private, local-only saves, and zero ads or monetization.
